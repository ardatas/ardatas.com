As part of volunteer work with the KACUV Foundation, I helped raise funds for a
cause supporting children with cancer and ran a marathon connected to that
effort.

The campaign raised more than 26,000 TL and required sustained outreach,
coordination, and commitment beyond a single event.

This project is included here because technical portfolios should still show
what kind of teammate and community member a person is. Engineering work often
demands persistence, responsibility, and follow-through; fundraising and
volunteer work exercise those same habits in a different setting.

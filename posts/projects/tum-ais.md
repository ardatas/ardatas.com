I work as a student assistant at TUM's Institute of Automation and Information
Systems in Munich.

I am developing a desktop application for visually building and running
industrial time-series processing workflows. The application uses Python,
PySide6, NumPy, SciPy, and HDF5.

The pipeline covers CSV-to-HDF5 ingestion, signal transformation, resampling,
segmentation, change-point detection, and result visualization. The aim is to
make each processing step visible and configurable in one desktop workflow.

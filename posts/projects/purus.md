Full-stack booking and customer-management platform for a mobile detailing business.

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL, with Supabase authentication, role-based dashboards, automated email notifications, and calendar exports.

Structured as a monorepo with independently deployable frontend and backend apps on Google Cloud Run.

At TUM Blockchain Club, I contribute as an active member of the External Affairs
department.

The role sits between technical community building and organizational
coordination. External affairs work requires clear communication, careful
follow-through, and the ability to represent a technical student group in a
professional context.

It is also a useful complement to engineering work. Software projects often
fail or succeed based on coordination: who the users are, which partners matter,
and how clearly the team can explain what it is building.

This experience keeps that broader product and ecosystem perspective close to
the technical work.

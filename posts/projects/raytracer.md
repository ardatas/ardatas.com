I study Computer Science at Technical University of Munich, with coursework and
projects spanning programming, systems, software engineering, and applied
computing.

The academic focus pairs well with hands-on software work. Backend systems,
frontend products, and lower-level simulations all benefit from the same habit:
make assumptions visible, test behavior, and understand the tradeoffs behind a
design.

I am also involved as a student representative and student council member. That
work adds a different layer to the degree: listening to other students,
communicating clearly, and helping improve the environment around the technical
work.

The long-term goal is to keep combining rigorous computer science fundamentals
with useful, shipped software.
